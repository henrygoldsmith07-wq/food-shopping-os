# High Taste Apps → Forq UI and onboarding

Reviewed 10 November 2026. Source: [Jaimin's collection](https://appllama.io/u/jaimin/high-taste-apps).

## Evidence and limits

Opened all 42 app pages and inspected a public App Store promotional screenshot for each, arranged into three temporary browser contact sheets. Read each page's available flow labels. These are curated promotional views, not hands-on app sessions. Most detailed onboarding and interior screens are Pro-locked; no locked content was accessed. The takeaways below are design interpretations of public imagery and flow labels, not claims about conversion, retention, accessibility, or the apps' actual first-run behavior. Long flow counts are not evidence that Forq needs more screens.

## Applied locally

- Starting goals now have a compact, goal-specific route preview. It explains steps without seeding imaginary meals, stock or savings.
- Account access follows the goal/name fields and is a native disclosure, closed by default. Auth callback errors and verification returns open it; authenticated accounts stay visible.
- Removed name autofocus so the mobile keyboard does not interrupt goal selection before the person requests text entry.
- Hide the large mobile marketing showcase after step one, keeping attention on the current task. Desktop keeps the existing editorial panel.
- Corrected the form section's flex direction: the form, recovery and privacy siblings previously shared a horizontal flex row. They now stack vertically with a shrink-safe content column. This is a verified source layout defect, not proof of the historical CI timeout's cause.
- Navigation uses a sticky, opaque footer with safe-area spacing and no entry animation. Advancing focuses the step heading for keyboard/screen-reader orientation.

No account/persistence/sync or meal-generation behavior was deliberately changed. Existing unrelated edits and deletions were preserved. No commit, push or deployment is part of this request.
