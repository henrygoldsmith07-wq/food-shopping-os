FAQ

Do you track prices live?

No — receipt prices are recorded, retailer hub links you to official shops for today's price/stock.

Is there cloud?

Optional: Auth.js + Upstash Redis household sync. Without env vars it stays 100% local.

Can I barcode scan?

Optional Open Food Facts lookup (signed-in, explicit action) for identity/allergens/nutrition.
